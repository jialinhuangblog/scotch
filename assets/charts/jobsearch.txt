JOBSEARCH PORTAL (SEA) — MOCK INTERVIEW SCRIPT
===============================================

題目給了一整張需求清單，搜尋、六個 9、A/B、三種通知、分析，看起來每一條都要
一個大元件。算完量之後，流量其實很小。這題考的是把每條需求接到同一條 CDC 串流
上，還有敢不敢對六個 9 提出質疑。對白英文，場景標題跟導演註解（>）中文。


SCENE 0 — Opening
【現在在做：題目沒講的地方先問，不登入能不能搜、刊登後多快搜得到、職缺有幾筆、六個 9 能不能談】
------------------------------------------------------------
INTERVIEWER: Our client wants a job search portal for Southeast Asia.
Thailand, Vietnam, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia to start. Free and premium
tiers for both job seekers and employers. You have the requirements sheet.

CANDIDATE: A few questions before I draw. First: can a job seeker search
without logging in?

INTERVIEWER: Yes, search should be open.

CANDIDATE: Then job pages are public content, the same HTML for everyone,
and I can render them on the server and cache them at the CDN. It also
means search engines can index every posting, which for a job site is free
traffic.

Second: when an employer posts a job, how fast must it show up in search?

INTERVIEWER: Within a few seconds.

CANDIDATE: Seconds, not milliseconds. That lets me update the search index
asynchronously instead of inside the employer's write request.

Third: how many active postings, and do we store resumes?

INTERVIEWER: Hundreds of thousands of postings. Resumes are stored. Employers
searching resumes is out of scope for now.

CANDIDATE: Last one. The sheet says six nines. Is that a hard number, or is
it open to discussion with the client?

INTERVIEWER: What makes you ask?

CANDIDATE: I'll show the math in a minute. It changes the deployment more
than anything else on the sheet.

> 不登入 → SSR 加 CDN；幾秒內 → 非同步建索引；幾十萬筆 → 搜尋 cluster 不用大；六個 9 → 要不要開第二個區域。問完就知道哪幾塊是實線，
> 哪幾塊只是開放討論的虛線。


SCENE 1 — Functional requirements
【現在在做：把需求清單收成五條，範圍外的當場講出來】
----------------------------------------------------
CANDIDATE:

  1. Search jobs by title, skills and description, with filters
  2. Employers post and edit jobs, seekers apply with a resume
  3. Notifications by SMS, email and WhatsApp
  4. A/B test UI changes, for all users or a targeted subset
  5. Near real-time sales analytics for forecasting

  Out of scope: resume search by employers, languages other than English
  for now

INTERVIEWER: Where do free and premium differ?

CANDIDATE: For employers, premium postings rank higher and stay up longer.
For seekers, premium could mean early access to new postings or seeing who
viewed their profile. Architecturally it's a tier field on the user and a
boost in ranking, not a separate system. The subscription table matters
more for requirement 5, because that's the sales data being forecast.

> 付費方案很容易被畫成另一套系統。講成「user 上一個 tier 欄位，排序時加權」，
> 再順手接到分析那條需求，同一張訂閱表就是業務要預測的資料。


SCENE 2 — Non-functional requirements
【現在在做：把量算出來，然後對六個 9 提出質疑】
------------------------------------------------
CANDIDATE: The sheet says 500 thousand daily users, peaking at a million.
Assume each user makes about 30 requests a day, searches, page views,
applications. At the peak that's 30 million requests a day, roughly 350 per
second on average. Traffic isn't flat, so call the peak five times that,
around 1,700 per second.
  100 萬人 × 每人 30 次 = 3,000 萬次／天
  3,000 萬 ÷ 86,400 秒 ≈ 347 次／秒 ≈ 350 次／秒
  347 × 5（尖峰）≈ 1,736 ≈ 1,700 次／秒

That's small. A few stateless app servers handle it, and most of it never
reaches them because public pages come out of the CDN. Scaling isn't what
makes this problem hard.

Now six nines. 99.9999% of a year is about 31 seconds of downtime, total,
for the whole year. One bad deploy uses that up. A failover that takes two
minutes uses it up four times over (3.8).

  365 天 × 86,400 秒 = 31,536,000 秒／年
  100% − 99.9999% = 0.0001%
  31,536,000 秒 × 0.0001% ≈ 31.5 秒
  2 分鐘 = 120 秒
  120 秒 ÷ 31.5 秒 ≈ 3.8 ≈ 4 次

INTERVIEWER: So you can't do it?

CANDIDATE: I can, but it has a price the client should see before signing.
It means two regions both serving live traffic, so losing one region is not
an outage. That doubles the infrastructure and adds cross-region data sync.
I'd design for four nines in one region with multiple availability zones,
and show six nines as the upgrade path with its cost attached.

  - Search results under one second
  - New postings searchable within seconds
  - Analytics must not load the production database
  - Four nines by default, six nines as a priced option

> 1,700 QPS 那一段要把假設講出口（每人 30 個 request），面試官可以改假設，但不會
> 說他亂算。六個 9 換成「一年 31 秒」，客戶一聽就懂代價。質疑完把價格攤開，讓客戶
> 自己選，這一段的分數在這裡。


SCENE 3 — The first version, and why search moves out
【現在在做：v1 只有一條路，然後指出它哪裡會出問題】
----------------------------------------------------
CANDIDATE: The simplest version that works: browser, CDN, load balancer,
app servers, one Postgres. Search runs as Postgres full-text search on the
same database.

At our traffic, that runs. The problem is what shares the machine. Search
queries match three text fields and sort by relevance, and they run on the
same primary that takes every employer's write. A burst of searches slows
down posting a job, and the two have nothing to do with each other.

So search moves out. Postgres stays the source of truth. A change data
capture tool, Debezium, reads the Postgres write-ahead log and publishes
every row change to Kafka. An indexer consumes the job changes and writes
them into Elasticsearch. A posting is searchable a few seconds after it's
saved, which matches the requirement.

INTERVIEWER: Why not have the app write to Elasticsearch directly?

CANDIDATE: Then every write goes to two systems, and one of them can fail
after the other succeeded. Postgres says the job exists, the index says it
doesn't, and nothing notices. With CDC there's one write. The index is
derived from the log, and if the indexer crashes, the events are still in
Kafka, it resumes from its last offset.

> 先畫一版能跑的，再指出它的問題出在「搜尋和刊登搶同一台機器」，不是流量大。
> 面試官問為什麼不雙寫，因為雙寫的話，Postgres 有這筆職缺、Elasticsearch 卻沒有，而且沒人發現。CDC 只寫一次，
> 索引是從 log 推出來的。


SCENE 4 — Search in detail
【現在在做：sub-second 怎麼做到，還有雇主剛刊登完要看到自己的職缺】
------------------------------------------------------------------
CANDIDATE: Hundreds of thousands of postings is a small index. A few shards,
a replica each, and a query across title, skills and description comes back
well under a second. Premium postings get a ranking boost in the query.

For the hottest queries, "software engineer Singapore" hit many times a
second, results go into Redis for 30 seconds. Most of those requests never
reach Elasticsearch.

INTERVIEWER: An employer posts a job and immediately checks the listing.
It's not there. What happened?

CANDIDATE: They read through search, and search is a few seconds behind.
The fix is to not send that read through search at all. The employer
dashboard reads their own postings from the Postgres primary, so their new
job is there the moment they saved it. Job seekers see it a few seconds
later through search, and nobody notices that delay.

> 「剛寫完馬上讀」只發生在雇主自己身上，所以只讓雇主後台讀 primary，不必把整個
> 搜尋改成強一致。這跟 Bitly 處理剛建立的短網址是同一個做法。


SCENE 5 — Notifications
【現在在做：三個通道分開，一個供應商出問題不影響另外兩個】
----------------------------------------------------------
CANDIDATE: Application received, interview invitation, new matching jobs.
These are events, and they're already in Kafka, because the application
row went through CDC. A notification service consumes them, looks up which
channels the user opted into, and hands each message to a per-channel
worker: SMS, email, WhatsApp.

INTERVIEWER: Why split the workers?

CANDIDATE: The providers fail independently. If the WhatsApp provider is
rate limiting us or down, its worker backs off and retries, while email
keeps sending. One shared worker would let a slow provider hold up every
channel.

Every notification carries an id, and the worker records what it sent.
A retry after a timeout checks the id first, so the user doesn't get the
same SMS three times.

> 通知不需要新的事件來源，投遞本來就寫進 Postgres，CDC 已經把它送進 Kafka。
> worker 照通道拆開的理由要講成故障情境：WhatsApp 那邊慢，Email 照常寄。


SCENE 6 — Analytics without touching production
【現在在做：「without affecting the systems」落到具體的資料路徑】
------------------------------------------------------------------
CANDIDATE: Sales data means subscriptions and payments. The same Kafka
stream carries those row changes. A second consumer loads them into an
analytics database, ClickHouse or a warehouse, within seconds to minutes.
That's near real time for forecasting.

The forecasting queries run there. They scan months of data and they never
touch Postgres. CDC reads the write-ahead log, which the database writes
anyway, so the production database doesn't do any extra query work for
analytics.

INTERVIEWER: Why not a read replica for analytics?

CANDIDATE: It works at first. But analytical queries are long scans, and
long queries on a replica can conflict with replication, so the replica
either falls behind or cancels the query. And the replica still has the
production schema, which is built for writes, not for aggregation. A
separate store built for scans is the cleaner line.

> 這題要的「不影響線上」，答案就是分析讀的不是 Postgres。replica 那一問要講出
> 具體的衝突：長查詢讓 replica 延遲或被取消，schema 也不是為了聚合設計的。


SCENE 7 — A/B testing
【現在在做：分組怎麼算，為什麼在 CDN 分組，以及快取命中率因此下降】
--------------------------------------------------------
CANDIDATE: A flag service decides the variant. Hash the user id, or an
anonymous cookie id for logged-out visitors, together with the experiment
id, mod 100. The same person always lands in the same bucket, with no
lookup table. "A specific subset" is a targeting rule on top, for example
country is Thailand and tier is premium.

INTERVIEWER: Where does that decision happen?

CANDIDATE: At the CDN edge, before the page renders. If the browser decides,
it first paints version A, then swaps to B. The layout jumps, Core Web
Vitals get worse, and the experiment now measures the jump as much as the
design.

Deciding at the edge has a cost for caching. The CDN has to cache one copy
per variant, so the bucket goes into the cache key. Two or three variants
are fine. Twenty concurrent experiments multiply the copies and the hit
rate drops, so the number of live experiments should stay small.

Every exposure is logged as an event into the same pipeline, and conversion
per variant is a query in the analytics store.

> A/B 是前後端交界的題目。後端講分組，前端補上「在瀏覽器分組會先出 A 版再跳 B 版」，
> 再接到 CDN 快取 key 要多存幾份的代價。


SCENE 8 — Availability
【現在在做：預設四個 9 的做法，還有要做到六個 9 得多加什麼】
------------------------------------------------------------
CANDIDATE: Default design: app servers spread across availability zones in
one region. Postgres primary with a synchronous replica in another zone, so
a zone failure loses no committed data and fails over. Kafka and
Elasticsearch are replicated across zones too.

For six nines, a second region, say Singapore and Jakarta, both serving
live traffic. A global load balancer sends each user to the nearest
healthy region, and when one region fails, its users move to the other,
which is already running and already warm.

INTERVIEWER: What's the hard part?

CANDIDATE: Writes in two places. If an employer edits the same posting in
both regions at once, someone has to win. Job postings have a single
owner, so the simplest rule is that each employer's writes go to their home
region, and the other region replicates. Reads are served everywhere. That
avoids conflicting writes almost entirely, at the cost of slightly slower
writes for an employer who happens to be far from home.

> 第二個區域是 SCENE 2 講好的升級選項，這裡把它的代價講清楚。雙區域的難處在兩邊
> 同時寫入，「每個雇主的寫入固定在一個區域」是一個具體可行的規則。


SCENE 9 — Frontend lens
【現在在做：三個頁面、三種 state、SEA 的手機網路】
--------------------------------------------------
CANDIDATE: Three page types. The search page and the job detail page are
public, server rendered and cached at the CDN. The employer dashboard needs
login and no SEO, so it's client rendered and shipped as a separate bundle.
A job seeker on a phone never downloads the employer code.

State split. The search query, skills, country and page number live in the
URL. A search can be shared as a link, and the back button returns to the
same filters. Search results are server state, held in a query cache like
react-query, which also collapses duplicate requests and prefetches the next
page. Whether the filter panel is open is client state and nobody needs it
after a reload.

The search box waits 300 milliseconds after typing stops before it sends a
request, and cancels the previous request when a new one starts. Otherwise
a slow old response can arrive last and overwrite the newer results. While
new results load, the old ones stay on screen instead of flashing empty.

The job detail page carries schema.org JobPosting structured data, which is
what gets postings into Google's job search.

INTERVIEWER: Accessibility?

CANDIDATE: The search suggestions follow the ARIA combobox pattern, so arrow
keys move through them and a screen reader announces the selected one. The
application form has a real label on every field, and errors are tied to
their field with aria-describedby. The premium badge on a job card uses text,
not just color, so a color-blind user can tell it apart.

And localization: English for now, but currency and dates follow the user's
country, baht in Thailand, dong in Vietnam. Strings are in translation files
from day one, so adding Thai later is translation work, not a code change.

> 前端這段的決定都接回後端：公開頁面 SSR 是因為 SCENE 0 問到不登入也能搜；
> 搜尋條件放 URL 是因為可以分享；取消舊 request 是 sub-second 在瀏覽器那一端的
> 做法。a11y 講具體的元件跟屬性，不要只講「會注意無障礙」。


SCENE 10 — Wrapping up
【現在在做：三個開放討論，各自說清楚什麼條件下才加】
----------------------------------------------------
CANDIDATE: Three things I'd bring back to the client.

Data residency. If Indonesia or Vietnam require personal data to stay in the
country, each country gets its own database for users and resumes, and the
app routes by the user's country. Job postings are public and can stay in a
shared search cluster.

Thai and Vietnamese search. English splits words on spaces. Thai has no
spaces between words and needs a dedicated tokenizer. Vietnamese needs tone
marks handled, so a search for "viec lam" still finds "việc làm".

Resume search for employers. The same indexer consumes resume changes into a
second index, with fewer searchable fields than a job posting, no phone
numbers in the index.

INTERVIEWER: That's time.


跟其他題的差別
================

其他題通常有一個元件是整題的重點，Uber 是派單的鎖，Bitly 是發號。這題沒有：流量
尖峰約 1,700 QPS，幾台機器就夠。這題考的是把一張長長的需求清單接成一條資料路徑，
Postgres 寫一次，CDC 送進 Kafka，搜尋、通知、分析各自讀。

六個 9 是整張需求清單裡唯一要跟客戶談的一條。要做到一年只停 31 秒，就要兩個區域同時接流量，
做不做由客戶決定，面試裡要做的是把價格講清楚。
